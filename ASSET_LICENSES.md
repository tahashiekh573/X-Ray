**Changes made (as CC BY 4.0 requires):** the OBJ elements were merged per structure, converted
from millimetres to centimetres, re-oriented, fitted into MediaPipe's canonical face frame
(similarity transform plus soft-tissue-depth refinement), mesh-decimated, and re-expressed in
per-bone frames for the hand rig. The jaw is split at the temporomandibular hinge. The pipeline
is `scripts/assets/build_anatomy_assets.py`. The attribution appears in the app footer, in every
GLB (`asset.extras.attribution`) and in the manifest.

**Note on older file headers:** the 2011 OBJ files carry a header comment naming the previous
license, CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan. The database's current license page specifies CC BY 4.0 for the
database. This project follows the current license page. If your legal review prefers the
stricter historical terms, also release the derived GLBs under CC BY-SA. The source code is not
affected either way.

### Not available in BodyParts3D (reported limitation)

BodyParts3D has **no muscles of facial expression or mastication**: no masseter, temporalis,
orbicularis oculi or oris, frontalis, zygomaticus major or buccinator. This was verified by
searching both the IS-A and PART-OF parts tables. These muscles are therefore rendered as a
**schematic layer generated by this project** (see below) and are labelled "schematic" in the UI.
A future upgrade path is the Z-Anatomy model (CC BY-SA 4.0, which includes facial muscles). Using
it would need a Blender export step and share-alike licensing of the derived meshes.

## Test fixtures (not redistributed)

The browser tests play two images through Chrome's fake camera: `portrait.jpg` and
`thumb_up.jpg` from Google's public `mediapipe-assets` bucket, which MediaPipe's own test suite
uses. `web/e2e/fixtures/make-fixtures.mjs` downloads them on demand. They are git-ignored and are
**not** part of the application or its distribution.
